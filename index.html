Different paths, shared responsibility

Meet the people behind security

“Hacker” can mean many things. These broad categories describe different approaches to technology and security.

01

White hat

Authorized security professionals who help organizations find and fix weaknesses before they can be abused.

02

Red team

Specialists who simulate threats—with clear permission—to help teams improve their defenses.

03

Blue team

Defenders who monitor systems, investigate suspicious activity, and strengthen security over time.

04

Black hat

People who use technical skills without permission or for harm. Learn about this term in a responsible context.

Start with the fundamentals

Build skills the responsible way

Cybersecurity is a broad field built on curiosity, careful thinking, and respect for other people’s systems and data. Begin with networking, operating systems, and programming basics.

Practice only in environments designed for learning, such as your own lab or an explicitly authorized training platform.

Permission matters. Never test a system unless you own it or have clear authorization.
